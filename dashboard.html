| A LITTLE NOTE FROM QUEEN ZYRA

Honestly, I don't know how many of you would be interested in reading about ARMYs who are no longer with us, but I believe they deserve a place in this universe, too, don't they?

They were people who had their own lives, their own stories, and their own dreams. Some of them dreamed of meeting BTS someday, of seeing all seven members with their own eyes, and of telling them what their music meant to them. Sadly, not everyone gets the chance to fulfil every dream they carry.

That's why I created this little corner, "Until We Meet in Another Purple Universe." A place to remember those ARMYs, honour their stories, and give their memories a space in the universe they loved.

Because even though they are no longer here with us, their lives mattered, their dreams mattered, and their stories deserve to be remembered with kindness.

They were ARMY, too. And they deserve a place in our purple universe.

I don't know how many of you have read their stories, but I cried every time I found an ARMY, added their story, and learned more about them. I know I'll cry even more each time I add another story for an ARMY who is no longer with us.
