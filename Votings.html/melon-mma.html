2026 Melon Music Awards Voting

Global Top 10 voting guide for BTS

Global Top 10

International ARMYs can vote for BTS in the 2026 Melon Music Awards Global Top 10. Voting makes up 20% of the total winning criteria.

Deadline: November 13, 2026, at 11:59 PM KST.

Voting frequency: One vote per account per day. Multiple accounts may be used on one device only if allowed by the event's official rules.

How to vote

  1. Download the Berriz app from Google Play or the Apple App Store, or open the Melon MMA 2026 voting page in Berriz.
  2. Log in with your Google account.
  3. When prompted, select the K-pop Square community and create a nickname to set up your profile.
  4. Open the MMA 2026 Voting category, find BTS, and select Vote.
  5. Return daily to cast your one vote per account. Switch accounts only when permitted by the event's official rules.

Voting details and availability can change. Follow the event's official rules and notices.
